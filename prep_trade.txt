User needs logic to submit real orders.
We need to use: PostOrder request
URL: /rest/tinkoff.public.invest.api.contract.v1.OrdersService/PostOrder

Required fields for PostOrder:
- figi (or instrumentId)
- quantity (lots)
- price (optional for market orders)
- direction (ORDER_DIRECTION_BUY, ORDER_DIRECTION_SELL)
- accountId
- orderType (ORDER_TYPE_MARKET)
- orderId (unique string, e.g. uuid)

We also need to get accountId first.
URL: /rest/tinkoff.public.invest.api.contract.v1.UsersService/GetAccounts
